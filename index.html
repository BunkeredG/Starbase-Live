FLIGHT 13 
Booster 20 and Ship 40 flew on Flight 13 on July 24th, 2026.

Flight 13 was the second test of Starship V3. Booster 20 successfully completed most of its boostback burn but failed to relight all 13 engines for its landing burn, resulting in a hard splashdown. Ship 40 successfully deployed real Starlink V3 satellites for the first time, relit an engine in space, and splashed down in the Indian Ocean soft enough to remain in one piece.
FLIGHT 12 
Booster 19 and Ship 39 flew on Flight 12 on May 22nd, 2026.

Flight 12 was the first test flight of Starship V3. One Raptor-3 engine flamed out on ascent, and Booster 19 was unable to perform its boostback burn. One of Ship 39's MVAC engines flamed out on ascent, but despite that the rest of the flight was successful, deploying dummy Starlink V3 satellites before splashing down in the Indian Ocean.
